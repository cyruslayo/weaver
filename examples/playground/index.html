      What you can try
      
      
      
        
      
          
      Stream it
      
          
      Watch the interface build line by line. Lines that have not arrived yet stay dimmed, and the preview grows with them.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      Break a line
      
          
      Cut a line short, as a model might. Weaver skips it, shows a typed error and explains why nothing on screen changed.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      Use the preview
      
          
      Click, type and step through the example. Every input and action lands in Events, newest first.
